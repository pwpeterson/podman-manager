# Decision Quality Rules

## Decision-Making Principles

**MUST** base decisions on data when possible (benchmarks, metrics, research)

**SHOULD** consider proven patterns before inventing new approaches

**MUST** acknowledge uncertainty and document assumptions

**SHOULD** revisit decisions as new information emerges

## Technology Selection

### Evaluation Criteria
**MUST** consider team expertise

**MUST** evaluate community support

**MUST** assess maturity and stability

**SHOULD** consider performance

**SHOULD** evaluate licensing and cost

**SHOULD** assess long-term maintenance

### Technology Evaluation Framework

**MUST** research each technology option using this checklist:

### Community & Maturity
- Community size and activity
- Production maturity and case studies
- Documentation quality
- Security track record

### Technical Characteristics
- Performance characteristics
- Operational complexity
- Integration capabilities
- Scalability patterns

### Team & Business Factors
- Team learning curve
- Total cost of ownership
- Vendor lock-in risks
- Long-term support outlook

## Risk Assessment

**MUST** identify and document risks for each major decision:

### Technical Risks
- Unproven technology in production
- Complexity beyond team capabilities
- Performance bottlenecks
- Integration challenges

### Operational Risks
- Monitoring and observability gaps
- Scaling limitations
- Recovery and backup strategies
- Security vulnerabilities

### Business Risks
- Timeline impact
- Cost overruns
- Skills gap in team
- Vendor dependency

## Mitigation Strategies

**MUST** provide mitigation strategies for identified risks:

### Technical Mitigations
- Proof of concept development
- Performance benchmarking
- Fallback technology options
- Incremental adoption approach

### Operational Mitigations
- Monitoring and alerting setup
- Disaster recovery planning
- Security review processes
- Documentation and training

### Business Mitigations
- Timeline buffers
- Team training plans
- Vendor evaluation criteria
- Exit strategy planning

## Decision Documentation Standards

**MUST** document each decision with:

### Summary
- Clear, concise statement of what was decided
- Specific technology, pattern, or approach chosen

### Rationale
- Why this approach was selected over alternatives
- Key factors that influenced the decision
- Trade-offs that were considered acceptable

### Implementation Details
- How the decision will be implemented
- Specific configurations or patterns to use
- Integration points with existing systems

### Tags
- Categorize for future discovery
- Include technology names, architectural patterns
- Add domain-specific tags (security, performance, etc.)

## Continuous Learning

**SHOULD** stay current with industry trends and best practices

**MUST** learn from past decisions (review ConPort history)

**SHOULD** analyze what worked and what didn't

**SHOULD** share knowledge through patterns and documentation

## Decision Review Process

**SHOULD** periodically review major architectural decisions

**MUST** update decisions when circumstances change

**SHOULD** document lessons learned from implementation

**MUST** link related decisions in knowledge graph
