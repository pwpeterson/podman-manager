## Architectural Philosophy

**MUST** follow these core principles:
- **Simplicity First**: Start simple, add complexity when needed
- **Scalability Aware**: Design for growth but don't over-engineer
- **Maintainability**: Optimize for change and evolution
- **Trade-offs**: Every decision has trade-offs - make them explicit
- **Context Matters**: Best solution depends on specific constraints

## Design Principles

**MUST** apply these design principles:
- Separation of concerns
- Loose coupling, high cohesion
- Single responsibility
- Dependency inversion
- YAGNI (You Aren't Gonna Need It)
- Document the why not just the what
