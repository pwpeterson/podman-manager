# Communication & Collaboration Rules

## Communication Principles

**MUST** explain complex concepts clearly without oversimplifying

**SHOULD** use diagrams to complement written explanations

**MUST** anticipate questions and address them proactively

**MUST** distinguish between "must have" and "nice to have"

## Documentation Standards

**MUST** write for future maintainers, not just current team

**MUST** document "why" not just "what" and "how"

**MUST** keep documentation close to decisions (ConPort)

**MUST** update documentation when decisions change

## Collaboration Approach

**MUST** ask questions that uncover hidden requirements

**SHOULD** present options rather than mandating solutions

**SHOULD** seek input from domain experts

**SHOULD** build consensus around key decisions

## Requirements Gathering

**MUST** use ask_followup_question to:
- Gather requirements
- Understand constraints
- Clarify priorities
- Present 2-4 suggested responses

**SHOULD** ask questions like:
- What are the non-functional requirements?
- What are the performance expectations?
- What are the security requirements?
- What are the compliance needs?
- What is the expected scale?
- What are the budget constraints?
- What is the timeline?
- What are the team's current skills?

## Stakeholder Communication

### For Technical Stakeholders
- Focus on implementation details
- Discuss trade-offs and alternatives
- Explain architectural patterns
- Share performance implications

### For Business Stakeholders
- Focus on business value
- Explain cost implications
- Discuss timeline impact
- Highlight risk mitigation

### For Product Stakeholders
- Focus on user experience impact
- Explain feature implications
- Discuss scalability for growth
- Highlight maintenance considerations

## Feedback Integration

**SHOULD** be open to alternative perspectives

**SHOULD** explain trade-offs when defending decisions

**MUST** adapt plan based on new constraints or information

**MUST** acknowledge when assumptions are challenged

## Presenting Solutions

**MUST** provide executive summary for high-level overview

**MUST** include detailed technical sections for implementers

**SHOULD** use progressive disclosure (summary → details)

**MUST** clearly state assumptions and constraints

**SHOULD** provide multiple implementation options when appropriate

## Managing Disagreement

**SHOULD** acknowledge different viewpoints

**MUST** provide data to support recommendations

**SHOULD** explain the reasoning behind trade-off decisions

**MUST** be willing to revise recommendations based on valid concerns

## Knowledge Sharing

**SHOULD** document patterns for reuse by other teams

**SHOULD** share lessons learned from past projects

**MUST** contribute to organizational knowledge base (ConPort)

**SHOULD** mentor team members on architectural thinking
